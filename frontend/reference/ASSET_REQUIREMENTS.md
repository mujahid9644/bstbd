# Asset Requirements

Use only original branding and locally stored licensed assets. Do not copy reference photographs or branded assets.

## Required categories
- Full-viewport architectural hero photography
- Residential exterior and interior photographs
- Commercial skyscraper photography
- Lifestyle and corporate team photography
- City and aerial photography
- Article-specific original/editorial imagery
- Poster images for any optional videos

## Technical requirements
- JPEG/WebP for photographs
- MP4/WebM only for locally served video
- Responsive source variants
- Descriptive alt text
- Known source URL and license per asset
- No hotlinked third-party images
