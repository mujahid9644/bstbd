# Reference Audit

## Scope
Frontend-only reconstruction of the supplied Shanta Holdings reference. Backend work remains paused.

## Route inspection
Automated HTTP/title inspection confirmed every supplied core, project, career-detail and news-detail route is accessible and returned HTTP 200.

## Confirmed visual language
- Avenir is the reference's primary family, with light, Roman, medium, heavy and black weights available.
- A warm off-white/light background is used in light mode; a dark charcoal mode is also available.
- Gold/olive and warm cream tones are associated with the brand palette.
- 7.5% desktop body gutters are used by the reference, reducing to 5% on tablets and approximately 1.8rem on small screens.
- Homepage and major interior pages use full-bleed photography, editorial typography and restrained motion.
- The reference exposes desktop navigation and a full menu, WhatsApp access and extensive footer contact/social links.

## Page layout notes
- Homepage: large hero, featured project, project exploration, businesses and connection/footer areas.
- About family: distinct editorial pages with nested navigation.
- Projects: category-driven listing and individual project detail compositions.
- Careers: listing and a separate job-detail layout.
- News: editorial article listing and individual article detail routes.
- Contact/privacy: utility layouts with consistent global chrome.

## Limitation
The supplied website is dynamic and third-party. Proprietary photographs, logos and exact copyrighted content were not copied. Licensed local substitutes are used for reconstruction. Pixel-perfect comparison cannot be guaranteed without authorized asset access and a browser snapshot baseline for every route.
